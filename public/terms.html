1. What DOT DEED Does

DOT DEED lets you purchase domain name certificates as gifts. We facilitate domain registration through our registrar partner (Name.com) and arrange printing and shipping through our print partner (Prodigi). We are a platform connecting these services — we do not host domains ourselves or operate a printing facility.

All prices are in USD. Payment is processed securely through Stripe. Your order is not confirmed until payment is successfully completed.

By placing an order, you authorize us to charge the total amount shown at checkout, including the certificate service fee, print costs, shipping, and any applicable domain registration fees.

3. Domain Registration

When you purchase a domain as part of an order, DOT DEED registers the domain through Name.com on your behalf. The domain is registered in our account initially. Upon successful claim by the recipient (via the QR code on the certificate), we provide the EPP authorization code so the recipient can transfer the domain to any registrar of their choice.

Domain registration is subject to Name.com's terms and ICANN regulations. DOT DEED is not responsible for domain availability, pricing changes by the registrar, or any issues arising from the domain transfer process.

Certificates are printed and shipped by Prodigi, our print-on-demand partner. Shipping times are estimates and not guaranteed. DOT DEED is not liable for delays caused by the carrier or customs.

If your certificate arrives damaged, email Fraser@frasergarner.com with photos within 14 days and we will arrange a free reprint at no cost to you.

If your order is lost in transit, contact us and we will issue a free replacement.

5. Refunds & Cancellations

Within 1 hour of placing an order: Full refund, no questions asked. The print order will be cancelled with Prodigi.

After 1 hour: Once printing has begun, orders cannot be cancelled or refunded. Certificates are personalized with the recipient's name and domain and cannot be resold.

To request a cancellation or report an issue, email Fraser@frasergarner.com. Refund decisions for exceptional circumstances are made at our discretion.

6. Gifting & Domain Claims

The QR code on the certificate contains a unique claim link. Only the person who scans this code and completes the claim process can receive the domain transfer instructions. DOT DEED is not responsible if the QR code is shared with someone other than the intended recipient.

Once a domain has been claimed and the EPP code has been sent, the transfer is between the recipient and their chosen registrar. DOT DEED cannot reverse a domain transfer.
