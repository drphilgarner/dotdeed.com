The Importance of Registry ID

Why every certificate needs a verified identity.

What is a Registry ID?

A Registry ID is a unique, anti-forgery identifier assigned to every verified user of DOT DEED. It links the certificate holder to their domain and acts as a digital watermark of authenticity.

Why it matters

Without a Registry ID, anyone could claim ownership of any domain. The Registry ID ensures that your certificate is provably yours — backed by the DOT DEED registry and tied to your verified identity.

Anti-Forgery Protection

Each certificate without a Registry ID is marked as unofficial. A Registry ID adds a cryptographic layer that makes your certificate tamper-evident and officially recognized by the DOT DEED registry.

How to get yours

Signing up for a free DOT DEED account takes less than a minute. Once registered, you'll receive your personal Registry ID instantly. It will be automatically embedded in every certificate you create.
